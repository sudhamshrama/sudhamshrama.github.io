Supply-Chain Security

Signed Container Supply Chain

A cluster that refuses to run anything it cannot cryptographically verify, with no private key anywhere in the system. Syft produces a CycloneDX SBOM, a custom gate blocks on fixable findings with exceptions that expire, and cosign signs keylessly through Fulcio and Rekor. Kyverno verifies signature and SBOM attestation at admission in enforce mode, so unsigned images are rejected before they can run. The admission policies carry their own unit tests.
